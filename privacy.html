Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. About Green Healthy Week

Green Healthy Week is an educational website created as part of a school marketing project. It includes interactive games and activities designed to encourage children to learn about healthy eating and balanced food choices.

2. Information We Collect

The website does not require users to create accounts or provide personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers. We do not intentionally ask children to submit personal information.

3. Website Analytics

Green Healthy Week uses Google Analytics 4, a third-party analytics service, to understand how visitors use the website and improve its content and functionality.

Analytics information may include page views, interactions, approximate location, browser and device information, and session statistics. Google Analytics may use cookies and identifiers to measure website usage.

We do not intentionally use analytics to identify individual children or collect their personal contact information.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Google Analytics may use cookies to measure website activity. The website may also store certain game settings or progress locally on a visitor's device.

5. Third-Party Services

Google Analytics is operated by Google and processes information according to its own policies. For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy

Green Healthy Week is intended for children and families. We aim to minimize information collection and do not intentionally request personal information from children through the website.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the website's features or third-party services change.

8. Contact

Green Healthy Week is a student-created school project. For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the project creator.
